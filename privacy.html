Privacy

Privacy

BOTAN.IA respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you use this website, why it is collected, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

If you contact BOTAN.IA or submit the Build Your System form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, company information and details about the process you would like to discuss.

Please do not submit passwords, API keys or other sensitive credentials through the website.

Information collected through the website

This site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not store anything in your browser.

Like most websites, the services that host and operate it receive standard technical information as part of delivering a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. The website fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives a request from your browser when a page loads.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Evaluate whether BOTAN.IA may be able to help with a business process
  • Schedule and prepare for conversations
  • Operate and improve the website
  • Maintain appropriate business records

BOTAN.IA does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

The website is hosted on Netlify, which also processes submissions from the Build Your System form.

Fonts are served by Google Fonts. If you choose to book a conversation, the booking link opens Google Calendar appointment scheduling, which is also operated by Google under the same policy.

Data related to client projects

Information submitted through this public website is separate from the systems and data involved in a client implementation.

Data access, credentials, permissions and handling requirements for client projects are determined as part of the individual project.

Your choices

For privacy questions or requests, write to hello@botaniaco.net.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, the services it uses or legal requirements change. The date below reflects the most recent update.
