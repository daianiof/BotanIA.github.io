Oversight

Built with oversight in mind.

Systems should make work easier without removing control. BOTAN.IA designs clear review points, permissions and human oversight where they are needed.

  1. The system preparesIt gathers the information and prepares the next step.
  2. You decideReview, approve, edit or reject when your judgment is needed.
  3. The system continuesOnce the decision is made, the remaining steps continue automatically.

Not every action needs approval.
The level of autonomy is set around the work, the risk and your preferences.
