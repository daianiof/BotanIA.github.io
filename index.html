The four stages

Understand

We walk the operation with the people who run it. How work arrives, who touches it, where it waits, and what each step genuinely requires.

Design

We map what the agent will handle, what it connects to, where a person decides, and what happens when something goes wrong.

Build

We build against your live tools, starting with the path that carries the most volume. It runs alongside the manual process until the team trusts it.

Evolve

We monitor what we built, adjust it as volumes and teams shift, and extend it into the next area once the first one is holding.

Human approval

Where a person still decides

A system should not send things to clients or commit money on its own. Every system we build has approval points designed in from the start, and you choose where they go. You can see one in each of the examples above.

  • The system preparesIt gathers what is needed, reads it, drafts the output, and puts together the context behind the decision.
  • A person decidesYou see what it prepared and why. Approve, edit or reject. It takes seconds because the work is already done.
  • The system continuesEverything after that runs from your decision: sending, filing, updating and notifying, without another touch.
Working together

How we work

  • We build whole systemsA system takes on a complete process rather than a single task. Individual automations are parts of it.
  • You do not need to understand the technologyYou should not have to know what an API or an agent is. You understand your business, which is the harder half.
  • We build on the tools you haveWherever your existing software works, we connect it rather than replace it. New tools get introduced only when there is no reasonable path with what you already run.
  • One area at a timeWe start where the volume is, get it working in daily use, then extend to the next area. Starting small means your team can adjust as it goes.
  • What we need from youAn honest walkthrough of how the work moves today, access to the tools you already run, and one person who can answer questions. A few hours across the first couple of weeks, then very little.
  • What stays yoursIt runs in your accounts, on your tools, documented so your team understands it. Your tools, your process and the decisions that matter stay yours, and ongoing management is something you choose rather than something you are locked into.
Who it is for

When an agent is worth building

This work suits professional-service firms, agencies, clinics, trades and B2B businesses with a working operation and too much of it done by hand.

Technology can automate almost any task. Understanding how a business runs is what decides which tasks should be automated.

If a process runs every week, touches more than one application, and depends on a person to move it along, it is usually worth building. If it happens twice a year, it usually is not, and we will tell you that.

Start with a conversation

Tell us how the work moves today and where it slows down. If a system would help, we will show you what it would look like before anything gets built. If it would not, that is a fine outcome for a first call.
