Why I built BOTAN.IA

For more than a decade, I supported executives and leadership teams inside global organizations.

What I kept seeing

That experience showed me something that applies to businesses of every size. Disconnected systems quietly consume an enormous amount of time and mental space.

  • Information lives in different places.
  • Follow-up depends on someone remembering.
  • The same details get entered more than once.
  • Small tasks stay in your head because the systems around you are not carrying enough of the work.

Why BOTAN.IA exists

BOTAN.IA grew out of that problem. I wanted to take the thinking behind strong executive assistance and apply it to the systems behind a modern business.

Context, organization, anticipation, judgment and follow-through.

BOTAN.IA connects the work so business owners and teams do not have to carry every operational detail themselves.
