The four stages

How BOTANIA works

Understand

We learn how the work happens today, including where information gets stuck, tasks repeat, processes slow down or someone has to manually keep things moving.

Understand the operation before deciding what to automate.

Design

We map how the process should work, identify what is worth improving, and decide where automation, integrations or AI can help.

We prioritize what is worth changing and choose the right approach for the work.

Build

We connect the tools, build the workflows and AI agents, and test the system with real work before it becomes part of daily operations.

Automation for predictable work. AI where the process needs more intelligence.

Evolve

We monitor how the system performs, improve weak points and expand it as the business changes.

The system evolves with the business.

We don’t automate everything.
We automate what makes sense.

AI Agents

AI agents built for a role in your business.

Some work needs more than a fixed workflow. It may require information to be interpreted, several tools to be used, or multiple steps to be coordinated.

BOTANIA builds AI agents for that work.

Each agent is given a defined role, the tools and information it needs, and clear boundaries for when it can act independently and when your team should step in.

Lead qualification

Research & reporting

Internal knowledge

Operations

Trust & Safeguards

Control is designed into the system.

Giving AI the ability to work across business tools requires clear boundaries.

BOTANIA designs those boundaries from the beginning, including what an agent can access, what it can do independently, when approval is required, and what happens when something does not go as expected.

Defined access

Agents and automations are given access to the tools and information required for their role, rather than unnecessary access across the business.

Human approval

Important decisions can stay with your team. Approval points can be added around sensitive communication, financial commitments, unusual requests or other higher-risk actions.

Testing & exceptions

Systems are tested before becoming part of daily operations, including how they respond when information is missing, a connection fails or an AI result is uncertain.

Monitoring & improvement

Once a system is running, we monitor how it performs, identify weak points and improve it as the business changes.

  1. The system preparesIt gathers the information and prepares the next step.
  2. You decideReview, approve, edit or reject when your judgment is needed.
  3. The system continuesOnce the decision is made, the remaining steps continue automatically.

Not every action requires approval.
The level of autonomy is designed around the work, the risk and your preferences.

Technology & Integrations

Your tools should work together.

Most businesses already have the software they need. The problem is often what happens between those tools.

Information gets copied manually, updates live in different places, and someone has to keep the process moving from one system to another.

BOTANIA connects those tools through integrations, APIs, automation and AI so information can move through the operation without relying on constant manual coordination.

When the work requires interpretation, reasoning or coordination across several steps, AI agents can work across those connections as part of the larger system.
