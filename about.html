About BOTAN.IA

BOTAN.IA provides intelligent assistance for modern businesses.

We automate recurring operational work, build intelligent assistants and agents, connect systems and information, and manage those systems over time.

The aim is less manual coordination and more capacity for clients, delivery and growth.

Why BOTAN.IA

Built for systems that need to grow together.

BOTAN.IA takes its name from botany, the study of how living systems grow, adapt and depend on the environment around them. The .IA stands for Intelligent Assistance.

Businesses work in a similar way. People, tools, information and processes are connected.

When those connections are clear, work moves more efficiently and the business has more capacity to grow.

BOTAN.IA builds intelligent assistance around that ecosystem, helping businesses automate recurring work, connect systems and create stronger operational structure.

Healthy growth depends on connected systems.

Our approach

What BOTAN.IA does

  • Automation and integrationsRules and integrations handle work that can move reliably between the tools a business already uses.
  • Intelligent assistants and agentsAssistants handle work that needs more than a fixed rule, such as reading, sorting, summarizing and preparing information, or coordinating several steps. Each one has a defined role, access to the tools it needs and clear limits on when a person should step in.
  • Management and improvementExisting agents, automations and integrations are reviewed, maintained and updated as the business changes, including systems BOTAN.IA did not build.
  • Human oversightReview points and permissions are designed in from the start, so important decisions stay with your team.
Founder

Daiani Farias

For more than a decade, I supported executives and leadership teams inside large global companies. Most recently, I worked in the Office of the President and CEO at Qualcomm, with earlier experience at Google through Vaco, Baker Tilly, KPMG and BASF.

That work gave me a close view of how requests, information, approvals and decisions move through organizations, and how much of it depends on someone manually keeping the process moving.

At Google, I also led remote teams across India, Portugal and California, including training and quality assurance. Working across three continents required clear processes, consistent standards and the right checkpoints.

BOTAN.IA brings that approach to modern business systems: remove repetitive work and keep human judgment where it matters.

Based in La Jolla, California, working with businesses anywhere.
