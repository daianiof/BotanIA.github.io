About

Grow.
Evolve.
Adapt.

BOTANIA is named for botany, the study of how living systems grow.

Living systems do not grow by force. They grow by adapting to conditions, connecting to what sustains them, and developing what they need for what comes next. Nothing in a healthy system grows in isolation.

People work the same way. So do careers, ideas, and businesses. BOTANIA exists to create the conditions and systems that let that growth happen intentionally.

The founder

Hi, I'm Daiani Farias

For more than a decade I have worked in executive support, operations, coordination, and training inside fast moving global organizations, most recently in the Office of the President and CEO at Qualcomm. Before that I led a contracted team at Google, and I have worked alongside senior leaders at Baker Tilly, BASF, and KPMG.

At Google, part of my work was developing training programs for new employees and supporting development and performance improvement for people already on the team. That was where the two halves of what I do now became one idea. Helping a person grow and helping an organization run better are the same skill applied at different scales.

That work taught me to understand how people and organizations actually operate, anticipate what is needed before it becomes urgent, identify friction, organize complex information, improve processes, and support people through change.

I am based in San Diego and work in English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
