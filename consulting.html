When a business starts to grow

Direction first, then infrastructure

Consulting helps define the business. Systems can help build the infrastructure that supports it.

Most new businesses do not need automation on day one. They need a clear offer, a defined customer, and a workable plan. Once the work becomes repetitive, that is usually the moment a system starts to earn its place.
